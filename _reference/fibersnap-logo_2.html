Concept notes

MarkA snap pea pod — three peas, one pod. Reads as fiber (a real high-fiber food) and as the name's "snap" without needing a camera-shutter cliché every logging app already uses.
WordmarkTwo weights, one color split — "Fiber" regular, "Snap" bold in pea green — makes the compound name easy to parse at a glance.
Tagline"Fiber, in a snap." — a real idiom (quick, easy), not just a name echo. Promises ease of logging, which is the exact thing competitor reviews complain no fiber app gets right.
TypefaceSpace Grotesk — geometric, a little unexpected, avoids the generic Inter-everywhere look.
